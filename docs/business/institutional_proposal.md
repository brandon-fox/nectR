# Institutional Business & Campus Sponsorship Proposal

## Executive Summary

Universities spend heavily on centralized tutoring centers with static hourly wages, low peer-to-peer visibility, and high bottlenecks during midterms and finals. **nectR** replaces static budgets with a **Sponsored Seed Comb**:

1. **The Money Multiplier of a Seed Comb:** Every `1 nectR` injected as `RAW_POLLEN` by a sponsoring university first funds a session with a Faculty-Queen-Certified Worker Bee, then converts into `LIQUID_NECTR` that circulates 5x–10x across the student body at zero marginal cost to the university.
2. **Verifiable Academic Telemetry:** Because every transaction on the private Hive blockchain carries a directional service proof (`Tutor -> Learner`, `Course Code`, `Duration`, `Effectiveness`), departments gain real-time visibility into course bottlenecks and downstream student retention.
3. **Self-Sustaining Comb Growth & Organic Swarming:** As Queen service fees (certification, escrow bounties, cross-hive bridging) replenish the Comb, the Hive transitions from institutional sponsorship to self-sufficiency—and veteran student tutors graduate into Founding Queens of new Hives at partner institutions.
