# Behavioral Persona Swarm Specification (Tier-2 Sub-Agents)

To validate the economic theory empirically, **nectR** includes an Agent-Based Computational Economics (ACE) engine (`src/nectr/personas/`) where 6 persona sub-agent archetypes execute real signed transactions against the private blockchain.

## The 6 Behavioral Archetypes

1. **`FreshmanForagerPersona` (`RAW_POLLEN` Consumer $\rightarrow$ Emerging Peer Tutor)**
   - Draws initial `RAW_POLLEN` from the school-sponsored Seed Comb.
   - Spends `RAW_POLLEN` with `CertifiedSeniorWorkerPersona` in gateway courses (`CALC-101`, `CHEM-101`).
   - Unlocks peer-tutoring capability in secondary skills once comprehension lineage increases.
2. **`CertifiedSeniorWorkerPersona` (Seed Tutor & Swarm Candidate)**
   - Accredited by a Founding Teacher-Queen.
   - High tutoring bandwidth; accumulates `LIQUID_NECTR` and tests graduation sinks or qualifies to swarm a daughter Hive as a new Queen.
3. **`FinalsWeekPanickerPersona` (Seasonal Surge Stress-Tester)**
   - Simulates Midterm (Epoch 7) and Finals (Epoch 14) 10x demand spikes to validate Comb Waggle Dance surge bounties.
4. **`SybilRoommateRingPersona` (Adversarial Wash-Trader)**
   - Attempts reciprocal `A <-> B` fake tutoring sessions to farm Accepted Status or Comb rewards; validates Worker Policing decay.
5. **`DriftingPollinatorPersona` (Cross-Hive Migrant)**
   - Arrives from a foreign Hive with locked `nectR-HIVE_A`; must tutor local Bees in `HIVE_B` to earn **Accepted Status** and trigger the Comb-to-Comb bridge.
6. **`QueenFacilitatorPersona` (Non-Tutoring Administrator)**
   - Certifies Workers, arbitrates disputed sessions, and tunes Comb bounties to maximize the Hive Velocity & Diversity Index.
