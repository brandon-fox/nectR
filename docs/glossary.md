# nectR Domain Glossary & Ubiquitous Language

Every term, economic equation, institutional compliance rule, and honeybee (*Apis mellifera*) metaphor used across **nectR** code, specs, and academic manuscripts is defined below.

## Economic, Consensus & Federation Terms

- **`MV = PQ` (Fisher's Equation of Exchange):** Fundamental closed-economy balance where **M** is circulating token supply (`RAW_POLLEN` + `LIQUID_NECTR`), **V** is transaction velocity (turnovers per epoch), **P** is average session price, and **Q** is real tutoring hours delivered.
- **`PoUW-PoW` (Proof-of-Useful-Work + Proof-of-Work):** Hybrid consensus in `src/nectr/chain/` where campus nodes pair cryptographic SHA-256/BLAKE2b difficulty with a verifiable transcript-oracle `UsefulComputeProof` (`ball_2018`, $\Delta_{\text{sec}} > 0$) committing the epoch's Knowledge Graph (`kg_root`), Gini coefficient, and anti-collusion velocity matrix, paid strictly from a capped Comb Compute Jar (`ADR-0007`).
- **Demurrage:** A gentle, pre-programmed negative interest rate (`~2%/month` default) on stagnant idle balances above a protected student safety cushion during Weeks 1–13 (paused during Finals Weeks 14–15), routed back to the Hive **Comb** to deter early-semester hoarding and subsidize Finals Week tutoring (`DEC-001`).
- **Burn-Mint Equilibrium (`BME`) & Collateralization Ratio (`CR`):** Treasury solvency guard ($CR = S_{\text{comb}} / L_{\text{outstanding}} \ge 1.0$) ensuring steady-state Sink-to-Source parity ($\text{SSR} = 1.00$).
- **Pairwise Discoordination & `COCM`:** Anti-collusion graph weighting ($1 / (1 + \alpha N_{u \leftrightarrow v})$) and Connection-Oriented Cluster Matching (using the Fiedler vector of dorm/club SBT graphs) that caps $m$-node roommate wash-trading below $\binom{m}{2}k$ while preserving full rewards for tutoring distinct classmates (`DEC-003`, `EXP-003`).
- **Spence Signalling (Downstream Mastery):** Replacing easily inflated public 5-star ratings (`filippas_2020`) with unforgeable single-crossing downstream outcomes ($s^* = 6$: whether a tutee subsequently passes mastery checks or teaches peers in the **Learn-then-Teach** cascade, `spence_1973`).
- **CQRS Knowledge Graph Isolation & NATS Leaf Nodes:** Architectural separation (`ADR-0014`) where consensus commits only a 32-byte `kg_root` and emits post-commit events to an off-chain `networkx`/`NetworKit`/`rustworkx` projector while federating `AcceptedStatusGranted` proofs across campus NATs via outbound **NATS Leaf Nodes (port `7422`)** and **JetStream Domains**.
- **Zero-PII FERPA/GDPR Boundary & Title IV Firewall:** Institutional compliance architecture (`ADR-0015`) keeping 100% of student PII in campus-hosted relational storage (anchoring only salted hashes or BBS+ Zero-Knowledge proofs on-chain), abstracting wallet keys behind **InCommon Shibboleth SAML 2.0** and **1EdTech LTI 1.3 (`NRPS`)**, and framing `LIQUID_NECTR` as non-monetary organizer-cleared service-duration credits to prevent IRS Pub 970 W-2/1099-NEC and federal Title IV financial-aid overaward violations.

## Biomimetic Hive Entities & Token States

- **`RAW_POLLEN`:** Institutional seed voucher drawn from the **Comb**; restricted so it can ONLY be spent with a Queen-certified Worker Bee in a certified subject, converting into `LIQUID_NECTR` upon verified settlement (`ADR-0006`, `D-05`).
- **`LIQUID_NECTR`:** Unrestricted peer-to-peer currency created only when `RAW_POLLEN` is refined via verified tutoring, freely transferable through `DirectionalServiceBarterContract` handshakes (`ADR-0002`, `D-01`).
- **`ROYAL_COMB_RESERVE` (`Comb`):** Shared Hive treasury holding seed capital, 100% of Queen governance fees, certification stakes, bridge tolls, and demurrage inflows to fund Waggle surge bounties and compute rebates (`ADR-0004`, `D-03`).
- **`Beekeeper`:** Institutional/campus root authority holding transparent, on-chain logged emergency override ("God Mode"), random meta-audit ($p = 0.15$), and bond-slashing powers over Hives (`ADR-0003`).
- **`Queen` & `Royal Jelly`:** Non-tutoring Hive administrator (Phase 1 Teacher-Queen earning non-coin impact credits or Phase 2 Veteran Student-Queen earning bond-backed `LIQUID_NECTR` dividends) who certifies Worker Bees, arbitrates disputes, and tunes Hive defaults—cryptographically barred from tutoring for `nectR` (`ADR-0003`, `ADR-0004`, `D-02`).
- **`Certified Worker Bee` (Nurse-Tutor):** Student tutor accredited by a Queen in specific subjects; eligible to refine `RAW_POLLEN` into `LIQUID_NECTR`.
- **`Learner Bee`:** Student onboarding into the Hive with `RAW_POLLEN` vouchers who can progress into peer tutoring after verified mastery.
- **`Pollinator Visitor` (`Scout`) & `Accepted Status`:** Visiting Bee migrating between Hives; locked home-Hive `nectR` cannot bridge until **Guard Bees** verify local tutoring contributions ($K=3$ distinct peers) in the destination Hive (`ADR-0005`, `D-04`).
- **`Waggle` / `Tremble` / `Stop-Signals`, `Ethyl Oleate (EO)` & `Swarming`:** Dynamic Comb `60%` surge co-pays during Midterm/Finals demand spikes (`Waggle`, `DEC-002`), latency-driven fee reallocation across `20s–80s` queue delays (`Tremble`, `DEC-013`), cross-inhibitory throttling of congested cohorts (`Stop-Signals`), Khoury collapse protection clamping Nurse-to-Scout promotion (`EO Circuit Breaker`, `D-07`), and mature Hives spinning off daughter Hives via a `6.25x–8.0x` Honey-to-Wax burn and 15-Scout Quorum Sensing (`Swarming`, `ADR-0006`, `DEC-012`, `D-08`).
