| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **The Wax Comb** | Shared caloric reserve buffering the colony against winter and scarcity. | `TokenStateAndCombContract` holds Hive reserves replenished by Queen service fees, Certification stakes, and Bridge tolls. |
| **Waggle Dance & Tremble Dance** | Recruiting foragers to high-yield patches; throttling overloaded queues. | Dynamic Comb surge bounties during Finals Week (`Waggle`) and fee/difficulty throttling on oversupplied topics (`Tremble`). |

| **Worker Policing** | Workers destroy rogue worker-laid eggs to prevent selfish cheating. | `KnowledgeGraphAndPolicingContract` decays pairwise wash-trading loops ($A \leftrightarrow B$) and slashes colluding pairs. |
| **Guard Bee Acceptance** | Guards reject foreign robber bees, but accept drifting foreign bees arriving laden with nectar. | `GuardBeeCrossHiveBridgeContract` locks foreign `nectR` until the visiting Bee completes $K$ verified tutoring sessions in the new Hive (**Accepted Status**). |
| **Reproductive Swarming** | Mature hive splits reserves and sends a Queen + workers to establish a daughter colony. | Veteran Certified Bees graduate to Royal Status and launch a daughter Hive seeded by a Swarm Grant from mature Combs. |
