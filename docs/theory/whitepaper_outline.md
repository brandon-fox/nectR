# Academic Whitepaper Blueprint: nectR

**Working Title:** *nectR: Biomimetic Mechanism Design, Graph-Theoretic Collusion Resistance, and Closed-Loop Monetary Equilibrium for Federated Peer-to-Peer Campus Economies*

This outline governs the publication manuscript in `paper/nectR_whitepaper.md`. Every section traces directly to verified literature in [`paper/references.yaml`](https://github.com/brandon-fox/nectR/blob/main/paper/references.yaml), formal provenance edges in [`paper/lineage.yaml`](https://github.com/brandon-fox/nectR/blob/main/paper/lineage.yaml) ([rendered report](provenance_lineage.md)), and reproducible simulation outputs in `experiments/`.

## Standard ABM `ODD+D` Protocol Mapping (`DEC-020` — `JASSS` / `JEBO` Appendix)

1. **Overview (`O`):** *Purpose* — test closed-loop tutoring liquidity, Sybil resistance, and colony stability across 15-week academic semesters; *Entities, State Variables & Scales* — `Learner`, `CertifiedWorker`, `Pollinator`, `Queen`, and `Comb` accounts quantified in exact `uint256` `micro_nectr` (`DEC-017`) and `0..10,000 bps` attestations (`DEC-018`) on zero-gas `vyper` + `py-evm` (`ADR-0017`); *Process Overview & Scheduling* — discrete weekly epochs (`Weeks 1–15`) with deterministic PRNG seeding.
2. **Design Concepts (`D`):** *Theoretical Background* — *Apis mellifera* foraging/policing ODEs paired with Pairwise Discoordination, Spence single-crossing signalling, and Principal-Supervisor-Agent auditing; *Individual Decision-Making (`+D`)* — boundedly rational utility + adaptive contextual bandit (`SybilRoommateRingPersona`) and reservation-price (`CertifiedSeniorWorkerPersona`) rules under heterogeneous information and endogenous network cues (`Waggle`, `Tremble`, `Stop-Signals`, `EO`).
3. **Details (`D`):** *Initialization* — Phase 1 Sponsored Seed Comb (`RAW_POLLEN`) + faculty Queen certifications; *Input Data* — empirical midterm/finals demand multipliers (`0.3x` $\rightarrow$ `10.0x`); *Submodels* — lazy demurrage (`DEC-001`), `60%` Waggle co-pay (`DEC-002`), Khoury mortality circuit breaker (`D-07`), and Guard Bee cross-Hive FX (`ADR-0005`).
