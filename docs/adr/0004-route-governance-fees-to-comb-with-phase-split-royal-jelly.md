# Route Governance Fees to Comb with Phase-Split Royal Jelly

## Context and Problem Statement

Taxing peer-to-peer tutoring depresses student trade velocity, whereas letting Queens pocket dispute or certification fees directly incentivizes rubber-stamping weak tutors and manufacturing disputes.

## Considered Options

* **0% P2P fee; 100% of governance fees routed to the Hive `Comb`; Royal Jelly split by Queen phase (non-coin impact credits for Phase 1 Teacher-Queens; bond-backed `LIQUID_NECTR` dividends for Phase 2 Student-Queens)**
* **Percentage tax on every P2P tutoring transaction**
* **Direct per-dispute and per-certification fee payouts to Queens**

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "0% P2P fee; 100% of governance fees routed to the Hive `Comb`; Royal Jelly split by Queen phase", because frictionless P2P swaps maximize circulation, Comb fee routing replenishes surge/compute reserves, Phase 1 faculty never drain student liquidity, and Phase 2 Student-Queens earn Comb growth shares tied to honest velocity, downstream mastery, and low dispute rates under a Beekeeper-slashable bond.

### Consequences

* Good, because honest Hive stewardship strictly dominates bribery and dispute-farming equilibria.
* Bad, because Comb solvency depends on calibrated governance sinks and idle-balance demurrage rather than P2P transaction taxes.
