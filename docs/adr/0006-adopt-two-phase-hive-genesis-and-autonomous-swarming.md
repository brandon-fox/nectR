# Adopt Two-Phase Hive Genesis and Autonomous Swarming

* Status: accepted
* Deciders: nectR Product Lifecycle & Tokenomics Architecture
* Date: 2026-11-21

## Context and Problem Statement

Campus barter networks face cold-start illiquidity at launch and overlapping-generations (OLG) currency drain every spring when credit-rich Seniors graduate with unspent balances.

## Considered Options

* **Phase 1 Sponsored Seed Comb (`RAW_POLLEN` -> `LIQUID_NECTR` via Certified Workers) + Phase 2 Autonomous Swarming + 3-door Senior graduation sinks and dormant alumni Comb sweeps**
* **Pure consumer LETS mutual credit with negative freshman overdrafts**
* **Continuous unbacked personal UBI token minting**

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Phase 1 Sponsored Seed Comb + Phase 2 Autonomous Swarming + 3-door Senior graduation sinks and dormant alumni Comb sweeps", because restricted `RAW_POLLEN` guarantees every liquid coin is backed by verified tutoring, while Senior off-ramps (Honors/Swarming a daughter Hive as Queen, Beekeeper campus perks, and Freshman Pay-It-Forward grants + dormant wallet sweeps) recycle upperclassman surpluses and seed organic expansion.

### Consequences

* Good, because cold-start liquidity, Sybil minting, and spring graduation deflation are solved within one closed-loop lifecycle.
* Bad, because Phase 1 launch requires an initial institutional or club Seed Comb sponsor.
