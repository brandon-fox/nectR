# Behavioral Persona Archetypes & Telemetry Metrics

## 1. The 6 Persona Sub-Agent Archetypes
1. `FreshmanForagerPersona`: Starts with restricted `RAW_POLLEN` from Seed Comb; seeks tutoring in gateway courses from Certified Workers; later tutors peers in secondary skills.
2. `CertifiedSeniorWorkerPersona`: Queen-certified tutor; high tutoring supply; tests graduation sinks, hoarding dynamics, and Swarm Queen eligibility.
3. `FinalsWeekPanickerPersona`: Hoards liquidity until Midterm/Finals epochs, then creates 10x demand spikes to stress-test Waggle Dance surge bounties.
4. `SybilRoommateRingPersona`: Adversarial colluders executing reciprocal `A <-> B` tutoring loops to test pairwise collusion decay ($W = \frac{1}{1 + \alpha N_{A \leftrightarrow B}}$) and Worker Policing.
5. `DriftingPollinatorPersona`: Migrates from `HIVE_A` to `HIVE_B` with locked `nectR-HIVE_A`; must complete verified local tutoring in `HIVE_B` to pass the Guard Bee Accepted Status threshold.
6. `QueenFacilitatorPersona`: Non-tutoring administrator; certifies workers, resolves disputes, and manages Comb bounties to maximize Hive Velocity & Diversity Index.

## 2. Closed-Loop Epoch Telemetry Metrics
- **Monetary Velocity ($V$)** and **Liquidity Distribution (Gini Coefficient)**
- **Comb Treasury Ratio** ($\text{Comb Reserve} / \text{Circulating Liquid nectR}$)
- **Collusion Attenuation Efficiency** (Effective Reputation gained by `SybilRoommateRingPersona` vs. Honest Bees)
- **Downstream Lineage Depth** ("Learn-then-Teach" multi-hop chains)
